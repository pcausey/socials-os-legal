Privacy Policy

Socials Dashboard

A personal analytics dashboard, built and operated by Peter Causey, that shows Lydia her own Instagram and TikTok metrics and the metrics for the brand accounts she manages.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the accounts explicitly connected to the dashboard by their owner or manager through each platform's own official login flow (Instagram Graph API, TikTok Login Kit). No account is ever connected without that owner's direct authorization.

What data is collected

  • Post-level metadata: captions, media type, post date, video duration.
  • Engagement metrics: views, likes, comments, shares, saves, and follower counts, pulled on a recurring schedule via each platform's official API.
  • Nothing beyond what the connected account's own analytics already show its owner — this dashboard reads the same numbers TikTok's and Instagram's own apps display, just aggregated in one place over time.

How it's stored

Data is stored in a private database on a server Peter operates directly. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or handed to any third party. Access is limited to Peter and the owner of each connected account.

How it's used

Solely to show the account owner their own performance trends — day-over-day views, engagement rate by content type, and similar rollups. An AI model may be used to tag a post's topic or format from its caption, purely to power the dashboard's own category views; this never leaves the private database or informs anything outside it.

Revoking access

Any connected account's owner can revoke access at any time from that platform's own app-permissions settings (TikTok: Settings → Manage account → Third-party apps; Instagram: linked via the Meta Business/App settings). Once revoked, no further data is pulled. Existing historical data can be deleted on request.

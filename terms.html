Socials Dashboard

A personal, non-commercial tool built and operated by Peter Causey for Lydia's own use.

What this is

This dashboard reads engagement data for Instagram and TikTok accounts, and displays it back to that account's own owner or manager. It is not offered as a public product or service to anyone outside the small set of people it's built for.

Who can connect an account

Only the person who owns or has management rights over a given account may connect it, and only through that platform's own official authorization flow. Connecting an account is the account owner's own decision, made directly with the platform, not with this tool.

No warranty

Provided as-is, with no guarantee of uptime, accuracy, or continued availability. Metrics are sourced from each platform's own API and may occasionally be incomplete or delayed where the platform itself limits what it exposes.
